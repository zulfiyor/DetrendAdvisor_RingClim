# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment
We are committed to creating a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for all participants.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent.
- Focus feedback on ideas and implementation details.
- Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.
- Personal attacks, insults, or trolling.
- Publishing private information without consent.

## Reporting
To report conduct concerns, open a private security advisory or contact maintainers through repository security/contact channels.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or block participation for behavior that violates this code.
